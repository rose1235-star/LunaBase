#  LunaBase
### An Interactive Lunar Exploration & Astronaut Mission Simulation

> **NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 — Junior Astronaut Mission Trainer**

LunaBase is an interactive lunar exploration simulation designed to introduce players to the challenges of operating on the Moon. Through an EVA mission, players experience astronaut procedures, life-support management, lunar environmental hazards, and scientific sample collection.

Rather than learning space science exclusively through explanations, LunaBase encourages players to understand it through gameplay, decisions, interactions, and mission consequences.

---

##  Project Overview

LunaBase places players in the role of an astronaut operating from a lunar research habitat.

Players must prepare for an Extravehicular Activity (EVA), follow airlock safety procedures, manage their spacesuit resources, navigate the lunar environment, collect scientific samples, and return safely to the habitat.

The simulation combines interactive gameplay with simplified representations of important lunar exploration concepts.

##  Mission Walkthrough

The current playable mission follows an astronaut EVA expedition.

1. Receive EVA authorization from Commander Reyes.
2. Enter the airlock chamber.
3. Close the inner hatch.
4. Perform the spacesuit safety check.
5. Depressurize the airlock.
6. Open the outer hatch and enter the lunar environment.
7. Explore the surface and collect four scientific samples.
8. Return to the habitat.
9. Repressurize the airlock.
10. Deliver samples to the science bench.
11. Review the mission debrief.

The mission includes scripted environmental events and survival challenges.

##  Scientific Concepts

LunaBase introduces players to:

| Concept | Gameplay Representation |
|---|---|
| Lunar gravity | Reduced-gravity movement |
| Vacuum | Airlock pressure procedures |
| Life support | Oxygen and water monitoring |
| Spacesuit systems | Oxygen, power, temperature, integrity |
| Radiation | Radiation exposure telemetry |
| Solar power | Solar arrays and habitat energy |
| Communication | Radio communication and signal blackout |
| Habitat protection | Shielded interior and survival systems |
| EVA safety | Airlock interlocks and suit checks |

**Scientific note:** LunaBase is an educational simulation. Several numerical values, environmental events, and survival mechanics are simplified gameplay assumptions rather than validated NASA operational parameters.

##  Installation and Setup

### Requirements

- Windows 11
- Godot 4.7.2
- Godot Compatibility renderer

### Running LunaBase

1. Download or clone this repository.
2. Extract the project if downloaded as a ZIP.
3. Open Godot 4.7.2.
4. Select **Import** in the Project Manager.
5. Locate `project.godot` inside the LunaBase project directory.
6. Import and open the project.
7. Press **F5** to launch the game.

Allow Godot to import resources on the first launch.

##  Development Status

LunaBase is an actively developed prototype.

The current build includes a playable mission loop and mission-success debrief.

### Verified during gameplay
- Mission progression.
- Sample collection.
- Mission completion.
- Mission statistics and debrief.
- Survival telemetry.
- Scripted mission events.

### Known limitations
- Astronaut models and animations are simplified.
- Some habitat exterior modules are not enterable.
- Several environmental and survival values are gameplay approximations.
- Some systems may require additional testing and refinement.
- Multiplayer functionality is not implemented.
